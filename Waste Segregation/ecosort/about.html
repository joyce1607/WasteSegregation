About EcoSort

Turning everyday waste into meaningful environmental impact.

EcoSort is an AI-powered smart waste management platform designed to make waste sorting easier, encourage responsible disposal, and show users what happens to their waste after collection.

Our Mission

To make responsible waste management simple, rewarding, and transparent.

Our Vision

A future where waste is not simply thrown away, but sorted, recovered, reused, recycled, or transformed into useful resources.

What We Do

  • AI identifies waste
  • Smart system sorts waste
  • Users receive Eco Points
  • Users can earn badges and certificates
  • Waste collection is tracked
  • Municipalities monitor waste
  • Recycling is tracked
  • Organic waste is processed
  • Suitable waste can go to energy recovery
  • Environmental impact is displayed

Our Process

Detect → Sort → Collect → Process → Recover → Impact

Why It Matters

EcoSort focuses not only on waste disposal but also on waste reduction, resource recovery, citizen participation, and environmental awareness. By creating transparency and offering rewards, we empower every individual to contribute meaningfully to a sustainable planet.
