How Can We Help?

Search our knowledge base or browse the FAQ below.

AI Scanner

How does waste scanning work?

The camera/image is analyzed and the system predicts the waste category based on our AI vision models.

What happens if AI is uncertain?

The system asks the user to manually confirm the correct category before sorting.

Eco Points

How do I earn Eco Points?

Earn points by correctly sorting waste and completing sustainability challenges.

Where can I see my points?

Go to your Dashboard, then click on the Eco Points section.

Leaderboard

How do the rankings work?

We feature both monthly and yearly rankings. The yearly leaderboard accumulates points throughout the year and identifies the Yearly Eco Champion.

Certificates

How do I get a digital certificate?

Users can unlock certificates by completing achievements and reaching significant Eco Point milestones.

Location

How do you use my location?

You can allow or deny location access. If denied, you can manually select a location to find nearby collection centers and processing partners.

Waste Alerts

What are waste alerts?

EcoSort identifies frequently generated waste from your profile and provides helpful suggestions to reduce it.

Where Does My Waste Go?

What is the waste journey?

Smart Bin → Collection → Processing Partner → Recycling / Organic Processing / Energy Recovery

Municipal Dashboard

What can municipalities see?

Municipal users can monitor waste collection, smart bins, recycling, organic waste, energy recovery, hazardous waste, and overall environmental impact.
